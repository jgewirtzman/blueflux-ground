# FCE LTER porewater nutrients (unmodified copy)

`FCE_LTER_1171_porewater_nutrients.csv` is the data entity of EDI package
knb-lter-fce.1171.16 (Castañeda-Moya E., Kominoski J. et al., "Monitoring of
nutrient and sulfide concentrations in porewaters of mangrove forests from the
Shark River Slough and Taylor Slough, Everglades National Park (FCE LTER),
Florida, USA, December 2000 - ongoing"; doi:10.6073/pasta/cd38181a01297bc25a086d71635a1a26;
CC BY 4.0), entity d99ac76b47918ea4050e7931ca574246, as downloaded from EDI
(pasta.lternet.edu) in September 2026. Kept verbatim because EDI now requires
a login for this entity. NH4, NO2, NO3, PO4 in umol/L; -9999 = missing.
Read by code/05_dataset/05_porewater_N_fce_context.R.
