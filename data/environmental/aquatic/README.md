# BlueFlux aquatic transect (dissolved gases)

`Everglades_Lateral_C_GHG_Dataset_export_2026-10-01.xlsx`: export of the Google
Sheet "Everglades Lateral C/GHG Dataset" (owner D. Vaughn, Utah State; shared
with J. Gewirtzman), downloaded 2026-10-01 through the Google Drive connector.
Tabs: BlueFlux Transect Data (119 stations x campaigns, Wet 2022 - Dry 2025;
pCH4/pCO2/pN2O in uatm, salinity, temperature, wind), BlueFlux ISCO Data (SRS 6
tidal series), River Area/Volume Calculations, Kate Data, Bibek/Bella Data.
Not edited. Used by `code/rebuild/11_water_flux_from_pch4.R` (stations SRS 5 and
SRS 6, next to the SRS5 / SRS6 flux plots). Re-export if the source sheet changes.
