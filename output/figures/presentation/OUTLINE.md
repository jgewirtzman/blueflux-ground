# Presentation outline — Mangrove CH4/CO2 budgets across a hurricane disturbance gradient
_12-minute conference talk. ~14 slides, ~50 s each. Figures in this folder._

---

### 1. Title  (~20 s)
- Title, authors, NASA BlueFlux, Florida Coastal Everglades.

### 2. Hook — why this matters  (~1 min)
- _Fig: none / concept._
- Mangroves = premier blue-carbon sinks, BUT they emit CH4 (GWP 28-80x CO2).
- Does CH4 offset the carbon benefit — and what happens when hurricanes kill them?
- Tropical cyclones intensifying; Irma (2017) killed vast Everglades mangrove -> "ghost forest."
- **Question:** how do component CH4/CO2 fluxes, and the net climate forcing, change from intact -> ghost forest?

### 3. Study system + disturbance gradient  (~1 min)
- _Fig: 03_study_sites.png_
- SW Florida (ENP + Big Cypress); gradient: healthy -> regenerating -> ghost.
- 8 sites, 3 seasonal campaigns (Mar 2022 dry, Oct 2022 wet, Mar 2023 dry).

### 4. Multi-scale approach  (~40 s)
- _Fig: 05_chambers.png (or a 3-panel concept)._
- Three independent scales: static chambers (components) -> TLS (surface area) -> aircraft (landscape).
- 867 chamber fluxes across 7 ecosystem components.

### 5. Workflow — chamber fluxes  (~40 s)
- _Fig: 05_chambers.png_
- 7 components: soil, water, prop root, stem, coarse woody debris, leaves, pneumatophores.

### 6. Workflow — terrestrial laser scanning (TLS)  (~40 s)
- _Fig: 06_tls_surface_areas.png (+ 06d_SA_by_height.png)_
- TLS gives component surface area per ground area; healthy stems ~0.9, roots ~0.2 m2/m2 vs ghost ~0.2 / ~0.03.
- Most woody (branch) surface area sits ABOVE the 1.5 m chamber limit -> need extrapolation.

### 7. Workflow — airborne eddy covariance (CARAFE)  (~40 s)
- _Fig: 07_aircraft_delaria-08.png (crop; cite Delaria et al. 2024)_
- Landscape top-down FCH4/FCO2 disaggregated by vegetation class (two end-members: intact vs ghost).

### 8. Flux results — component rates  (~1 min)
- _Fig: 08_flux_rates_CH4.png (+ 08b CO2; 08_..._by_season.png if time)_
- Soil + water highest; ghost/regenerating >> healthy for most components.
- Strong wet-season enhancement.

### 9. Diffusion vs ebullition  (~30 s)
- _Fig: 08c_diffusion_ebullition.png_
- Ebullition only in the WET season at disturbed classes (16% ghost, 8% regenerating); none at healthy.

### 10. Tree biology — the stem story  (~1 min)
- _Figs: 09f_stem_profile_CH4.png, 09c_alive_dead.png (09b species if time)_
- Stem CH4 declines sharply with height (transport-limited); CO2 flat with height (respiration).
- Ghost/regenerating stems emit ~20-24 at the base vs ~0.8 healthy.
- Dead A. germinans emits as much or more than living (height-controlled).

### 11. Geochemistry — the mechanism  (~50 s)
- _Fig: 10_geochem_pca.png (or 10b_salinity_ch4.png)_
- Porewater PCA: ghost sites = high salinity + high dissolved CH4 + sulfate.
- Salinity-CH4 relationship REVERSES: negative in healthy (sulfate suppression), POSITIVE in ghost (r=0.6, p=0.03).
- Disturbance overrides the classic salinity-suppression paradigm.

### 12. Scaling — chambers x TLS = budgets  (~1 min)
- _Figs: 11c->11f decomposition (rate x area -> integrated -> %), 11b_budget_stacked.png_
- Reveal the logic: flux rate x surface-area density -> integrated flux -> % contribution.
- Tide handling: 50/50 wet/dry surface (06e_tide_handling.png).
- Stem extrapolation: exponential fit, changes total <2% (09e_stem_extrapolation.png).
- **Result:** ghost budget ~99% water surface; healthy soil (69%) + roots (23%); stems <1% in both.

### 13. Budget closure — bottom-up vs top-down  (~1 min)
- _Figs: 12a_closure_CH4.png (+ 12b CO2)_
- Two fully independent methods AGREE: ghost > healthy CH4 in the wet season.
- Bottom-up ghost 37 vs CARAFE 51+/-27; healthy 6.9 vs 29+/-16 nmol m-2 s-1 (overlap within 1 sigma).
- Validates the chamber budget; refutes the reclassification that had inverted it.

### 14. Net radiative forcing — the payoff  (~1 min)
- _Fig: 13_gwp_forcing.png_
- Healthy = strong net SINK (-4,646 g CO2-eq m-2 yr-1, GWP100); matches SRS-6 tower NEP.
- Ghost = net SOURCE (+1,770); a ~6,400 g CO2-eq m-2 yr-1 cooling->warming switch.
- CH4 share of forcing rises from ~1% (healthy) to ~19% GWP100 / ~40% GWP20 (ghost).

### 15. Conclusions & what's next  (~50 s)
**Conclusions**
- Hurricane disturbance flips mangroves from a net radiative sink to a net source.
- Blue-carbon climate benefits are sensitive to disturbance state, not fixed.
- Nature-based solutions & GHG inventories should account for disturbance class + permanence risk.

**Next steps**
- Closing the full carbon budget: soil burial + lateral (tidal DIC) export.
- Updating IPCC emission factors by land-cover / disturbance class.
- Microbial metagenomes to explain hypersaline & disturbance-driven methanogenesis.

multi-scale flux approaches (chamber <-> TLS <-> airborne).
